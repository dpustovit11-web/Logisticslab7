Where people lose the most money

Most losses are not big mistakes. They are small details nobody explained. Our lessons show you how to catch them.

Unverified suppliers

Paying in advance to a seller you never checked is the most expensive mistake in international buying.

Wrong shipping method

The cheapest quote is often not the cheapest delivery once speed, size, and weight rules are applied.

Duties and taxes

Customs costs that are missing from the first price can erase your whole margin.

Hidden fees

Handling, storage, and documentation charges appear late. We teach you to ask about them first.

How to start a business with $0 in your pocket

No capital does not mean no start. Here is the path our lessons take you through, in order.

  1. Learn the market first

    Study what people already buy and how the supply chain behind it works. Knowledge costs nothing.

  2. Find a real supplier

    Use our checks to shortlist suppliers who are honest, responsive, and open to small first orders.

  3. Sell before you buy

    Collect interest or pre-orders first, so the customer's money funds the product.

  4. Ship smart and reinvest

    Pick the right logistics route, keep costs low, and put every early profit back into the next order.
